In `GCCBootstrap_jll` `9.4.0+0`, the affected target artifacts ship Binutils 2.38: `aarch64-linux-musl`, `armv6l-linux-gnueabihf`, `armv6l-linux-musleabihf`, `armv7l-linux-gnueabihf`, `armv7l-linux-musleabihf`, `i686-linux-gnu`, `i686-linux-musl`, `i686-w64-mingw32`, `powerpc64le-linux-gnu`, `x86_64-linux-gnu`, `x86_64-linux-musl`, `x86_64-w64-mingw32`. The `aarch64-linux-gnu` target artifact ships Binutils 2.29.1 and is outside the reported affected upstream range.

An issue was discovered function `stab_demangle_v3_arg` in stabs.c in Binutils 2.34 thru 2.38, allows attackers to cause a denial of service due to memory leaks.
